Floriva Privacy Policy

What information Floriva handles, how we use it, and what stays on your device.

Floriva is built without a backend for in-app reproductive-health data. Your cycle history, symptoms, moods, notes, TTC observations, and birth-control details stay on your device. Floriva does not receive them, retain them, sell them, or use them for analytics.

What We Collect On The Website

Floriva.app does not use PostHog, user analytics, broad autocapture, advertising SDKs, or tracking pixels for cycle tracking or in-app health activity. The marketing website does not load third-party feedback widgets. If Sentry error monitoring is configured, it may receive limited technical diagnostics so we can find broken pages or app errors. These website tools exclude cycle history, reproductive-health logs, notes, symptoms, moods, TTC observations, and birth-control details.

If you voluntarily request a free resource, Floriva collects the email address you submit, the resource requested, the page where the request was made, delivery status, and related email-delivery events. This information is used only to send the requested resource, prevent duplicate requests, maintain request status, and respond to privacy or deletion requests.

What Happens In The App

Your cycle history stays on the device it was recorded on. The Floriva app is built without a company server for reproductive-health logs and without an analytics or advertising pipeline for that data.

Health Data

Floriva is designed so reproductive-health logs, notes, symptoms, moods, cycle dates, TTC observations, and birth-control details remain local to the user's device. Floriva does not require an account for core use and does not sell reproductive-health data.

Data Retention

Floriva does not retain cycle history, symptoms, moods, notes, TTC observations, birth-control details, or other reproductive-health logs on company servers because Floriva does not receive that data. App data remains on the user's device unless the user removes it locally.

Free-resource email records are retained as needed to deliver the requested resource, prevent duplicate requests, maintain request status, and respond to privacy or deletion requests.

Diagnostics

Technical diagnostics, when enabled through Sentry, are limited to app or website error details and are configured without session replay and without default personal information. Diagnostics exclude reproductive logs, notes, symptoms, moods, TTC observations, and birth-control details.

Data Deletion

Because Floriva does not receive or store in-app reproductive-health data, there is no server-side copy for Floriva to delete. Users can delete local app data by deleting entries where supported, clearing app storage, or uninstalling the app.

To request deletion of free-resource email records, contact angel.campa@floriva.app from the email address used for the request.

Contact

Privacy and deletion questions can be sent to angel.campa@floriva.app.
