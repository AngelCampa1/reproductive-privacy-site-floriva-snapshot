### Why State Pages (50 TOFU)
SERP evidence confirms demand for state-level reproductive privacy content:
- Stateline.org published "Data privacy after Dobbs: Is period tracking safe?" (July 2024) — ranks for "period tracker delete data privacy"
- Duke Today published a study on "Data Privacy in the Post-Roe Era" (May 2024) — ranks for "menstrual data legal risk post roe"
- University of Maryland law review article "From Roe to Risk" (2025) — ranks for post-Roe data legal risk queries
- U.S. Rep. Sara Jacobs bill (March 2024) specifically targets state-level reproductive data law enforcement access
- FTC research paper "I Deleted It After the Overturn of Roe v. Wade" ranks #1 featured snippet for "period tracker app data privacy FTC"
- The search query "period tracker safe from government" appeared in SERP related searches on 2026-03-21
- Laws vary dramatically by state (California has CMIA; Texas/Alabama have restrictive abortion laws increasing prosecution risk) — state-specific pages have genuine informational value

- State pages (TOFU) link to: privacy guide "What period trackers are safe in [state]" (TOFU) + privacy-ranked listicle (MOFU) + Flo alternative (BOFU)
- Condition guides (TOFU) link to: relevant listicle "best period tracker for [condition]" (MOFU) + comparison page (MOFU)
- Comparison pages (MOFU) link to: respective alternative pages (BOFU) + pricing breakdown (MOFU) + relevant TOFU guide
- Alternative pages (BOFU) link to: comparison pages (MOFU) + pricing breakdown (MOFU) + product signup
