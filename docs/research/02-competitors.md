# Floriva — Competitor Landscape

**Product:** Floriva (floriva.app)

All competitors listed below were found ranking on page 1 of Google SERPs pulled on 2026-03-21 for the queries: "best period tracker app", "private period tracker app", "flo app alternative". No competitor data is fabricated.

---

### Flo Health
- **URL:** https://flo.health/
- **Privacy stance:** Historical cloud storage + data sharing with Facebook, Google, and third-party analytics. Settled FTC action in 2021 for sharing user health data without consent. Launched "Anonymous Mode" after settlement. Settled class action (Frasco v. Flo Health) in July 2025. Combined Google + Flo settlement of $56M reached September 2025. Still requires account creation.
- **Pricing:** Free tier available; Flo Premium subscription required for advanced features (exact price not confirmed by API; freemium model)
- **Key weakness for our angle:** Has a documented, high-profile history of sharing sensitive health data with Facebook and Google SDKs; subject to multiple FTC and class-action enforcement actions; requires email account; cloud-stores all user data
- **Sources:** Ranking #1 (App Store listing) and #7 (flo.health) for "best period tracker app"; ranking #9 and #12 for "private period tracker app" and "flo app alternative" respectively. FTC press release: https://www.ftc.gov/news-events/news/press-releases/2021/06/ftc-finalizes-order-flo-health-fertility-tracking-app-shared-sensitive-health-data-facebook-google. Reuters settlement: https://www.reuters.com/sustainability/boards-policy-regulation/google-flo-health-pay-56-million-period-tracking-app-privacy-case-2025-09-25/

---

### Clue
- **URL:** https://helloclue.com/
- **Privacy stance:** Based in Berlin, Germany; GDPR-compliant; states it never sells user data; works with scientists and researchers; cloud-based but claims strong data protection. Considered more private than Flo by Consumer Reports, Wired, and AllAboutCookies. Does require account creation.
- **Pricing:** Free tier; Clue Plus subscription for advanced features (exact price not confirmed by API)
- **Key weakness for our angle:** Still cloud-based; still requires account; GDPR compliance does not equal zero-knowledge; data is held on Clue's servers and subject to legal process in EU jurisdictions
- **Sources:** Ranking #5 (helloclue.com) for "best period tracker app"; ranking #8 for "private period tracker app"; ranking in "flo app alternative" AI overview. App Store: 10 million users per SERP snippet.

---

### Natural Cycles
- **URL:** https://www.naturalcycles.com/
- **Privacy stance:** FDA-cleared contraception app; cloud-based; requires account and personal health data including basal body temperature. Has a dedicated comparison page against Flo and Clue (naturalcycles.com/nc-vs-competition). Privacy posture not highlighted as a key differentiator.
- **Pricing:** Subscription required; exact price not confirmed by API
- **Key weakness for our angle:** Cloud-based; requires personal account; FDA-cleared positioning is for contraception, not privacy; data stored on Natural Cycles servers
- **Sources:** Ranking #9 for "flo app alternative" (naturalcycles.com/nc-vs-competition)

---

### Euki
- **URL:** https://eukiapp.com/ (app; also referenced via YouTube OBGYN video)
- **Privacy stance:** Local on-device storage only; no cloud sync; no account required; features a decoy/fake screen PIN to hide data from others; developed by a nonprofit organization focused on reproductive privacy; recommended by Consumer Reports, NordVPN, AllAboutCookies, and OBGYN Dr. Jennifer Lincoln. Free.
- **Pricing:** Free
- **Key weakness for our angle:** No cross-device sync; nonprofit with limited development resources; no web/desktop version; not widely known outside privacy-focused communities
- **Sources:** Ranking in AI Overview for "best period tracker app" (best for privacy); ranking in AI Overview for "private period tracker app" (top recommendation); referenced in Consumer Reports, NordVPN, hide.me articles. Found via SERP for all 3 competitor queries.

---

### Drip (Bloody Health)
- **URL:** https://bloodyhealth.gitlab.io/
- **Privacy stance:** Free and Open-Source Software (FOSS); supported by Mozilla Foundation; all data stored locally on device; transparent calculation algorithm; no third-party analytics; no account required. Consistently recommended by Consumer Reports, AllAboutCookies, hide.me.
- **Pricing:** Free (open source)
- **Key weakness for our angle:** Android-only; minimal UI/UX polish; no iOS version limits addressable market; FOSS maintenance dependent on volunteer contributors
- **Sources:** Ranking in AI Overview for "private period tracker app"; referenced in Consumer Reports (https://www.consumerreports.org/health/health-privacy/period-tracker-apps-privacy-a2278134145/); found via SERP for "private period tracker app" and "best period tracker no data sharing"

---

### Periodical
- **URL:** Found on Google Play (not a standalone website confirmed in SERPs)
- **Privacy stance:** Android app; local storage only; no internet access required; FOSS; highly rated in r/privacy subreddit ("I'd recommend Periodical. FOSS, no internet access needed, data stored locally, can be exported if needed."). Recommended by Consumer Reports alongside Drip and Euki as one of three top privacy-safe apps.
- **Pricing:** Free
- **Key weakness for our angle:** Android-only; no iOS version; no web presence; very limited feature set beyond basic tracking; no cross-device backup
- **Sources:** Ranking #2 for "private period tracker app" (Reddit r/privacy); referenced in Consumer Reports; AllAboutCookies; NordVPN articles.

---

### Stardust
- **URL:** https://www.stardust.app/ (referenced in SERP results)
- **Privacy stance:** Women-owned company; claims not to share user data; mentioned by Reddit users as a Flo alternative ("Stardust! It's a women owned company (unlike flo) and they don't share your data"). Wired analyzed Stardust privacy policy alongside Flo, Clue, Period Calendar, Period Tracker in 2022.
- **Pricing:** Free tier; subscription available
- **Key weakness for our angle:** Cloud-based; account required; privacy claims are marketing-level, not technical (not E2E encrypted or on-device); Wired ranked it below Clue for privacy
- **Sources:** Ranking #1 for "flo app alternative" (Reddit, r/TheGirlSurvivalGuide); Wired article: https://www.wired.com/story/period-tracking-apps-flo-clue-stardust-ranked-data-privacy/

### Spot On (Planned Parenthood Direct)
- **URL:** https://www.plannedparenthooddirect.org/spot-on-period-tracker
- **Privacy stance:** Developed by Planned Parenthood Direct; stated policy of not selling data or sharing with advertisers; judgment-free tracking for periods and birth control. Privacy commitment is policy-based, not technical.
- **Pricing:** Free
- **Key weakness for our angle:** Cloud-based; associated with Planned Parenthood brand (some users may avoid for unrelated reasons); no zero-knowledge or on-device storage architecture; privacy is a stated policy, not a cryptographic guarantee
- **Sources:** Referenced in AI Overview for "private period tracker app"; Planned Parenthood Direct page confirmed in SERP: https://www.plannedparenthooddirect.org/spot-on-period-tracker

### Cicle

### Period Tracker Period Calendar (Leap Fitness / GP Apps)
- **URL:** https://apps.apple.com/us/app/period-tracker-period-calendar/id896501514

### Bearable

- **Pricing:** Free tier; subscription for premium features (exact price not confirmed by API)
- **Key weakness for our angle:** Not purpose-built for privacy; positioned as symptom/mood tracker with period functionality; cloud-based
- **Sources:** Ranking #5 for "flo app alternative" (bearable.app); referenced in multiple SERP AI Overviews; has its own "best period tracker" roundup article at bearable.app/the-best-period-tracker-apps-of-2025/
