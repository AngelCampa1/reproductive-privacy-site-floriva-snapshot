Sleep can be hard to explain in a short visit. You may remember the worst night and forget the pattern.

This log puts sleep notes next to cycle notes. It covers trouble falling asleep, wake-ups, night sweats, and hot flashes in one place. It does not tell you the cause. It does not give sleep treatment advice.

It is not medical advice.

If safety is part of the issue, use your safety or crisis plan first. Do not wait to finish this sheet.

## Start here

Fill this in before the daily log.

| Field | Your notes |

If you do not know your cycle day, use plain timing. Try "3 days before bleeding" or "period day 2."

ACOG lists insomnia and fatigue as possible PMS symptoms. The Office on Women's Health lists sleep problems, hot flashes, and night sweats as symptoms that may occur during the menopause transition. Your log can show timing. It does not name the cause.

## Pattern check

Answer these after a week, after your period starts, or after a full cycle.

| Question | Your note |
|---|---|
| How many nights were hard? | |
| How many days before bleeding did it start? | |
| Did sleep change once bleeding started? | |
| Did night sweats or hot flashes happen too? | |
| Did fatigue or mood change too? | |
| Did this happen in past cycles? | |
| Did it happen far from period timing too? | |
| What do I want to ask? | |

The answer can be "not sure." That still helps.

## Questions for a clinician

Pick the questions that fit.

- Could my cycle timing matter?
- Could this fit a PMS or perimenopause pattern?
- What other sleep causes should we check?
- Should I keep a sleep diary for another cycle?
- Which symptoms mean I should call sooner?
- Should I bring a sleep summary instead of all notes?
- What private details can I leave out?

MedlinePlus suggests bringing questions and symptom notes to a visit. Keep your list short enough to use in the room.

## Privacy check before sharing

Sleep notes can include mood, sex, pregnancy worries, medicine names, conflict, location, work, and safety details.

Before you export, screenshot, print, email, or upload notes, ask:

- Who needs this?
- Do they need dates or the full log?
- Can I share a summary instead?
- Does it include private notes I can leave out?
- Will it go into a portal?
- Will it be saved in email or downloads?
