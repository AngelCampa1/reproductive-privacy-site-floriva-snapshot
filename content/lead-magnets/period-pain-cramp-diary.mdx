Period pain shows up in a lot of places. Lower belly. Back. Hip. Leg. Bladder area. Bowel. Rectum. Sometimes during or after sex.

Use this diary as one home for all of it. Pick the sections that match your symptoms. Skip the rest.

It is not medical advice. It does not diagnose the cause. It does not tell you what care to choose.

## Prompt-care check

Get care now if any of these fit, no matter which section you were about to fill out.

- [ ] Pain is sudden, severe, or getting worse.
- [ ] Pain is very different from your usual pattern.
- [ ] Fever.
- [ ] Vomiting, fainting, or dizziness.
- [ ] Heavy bleeding.
- [ ] Blood in urine or black stool.
- [ ] Pregnancy concern.
- [ ] New numbness or weakness.
- [ ] Symptoms that feel unsafe.

If you feel very sick, seek urgent care.

FDA and MedlinePlus note toxic shock syndrome as a rare but serious tampon-related risk. ## Heat and other comfort steps

MedlinePlus and ACOG name heat on the lower belly, exercise, hot baths, rest, and relaxation as things people may try for period pain. FDA notes that over-the-counter pain medicines are used in menstrual cramp products and that people should track pain, what it feels like, and where it hurts.

| Date | Body area | Heat source | Time used | Pain before | Pain after | Skin check |
|---|---|---|---|---:|---:|---|
| | lower belly / low back / hip / other | heating pad / hot water bottle / wrap / bath | | | | no change / red / sore / numb |
| | | | | | | |

This table is a record, not a plan. FDA also warns that pain medicines can cause serious harm when misused, and that NSAIDs should be avoided at 20 weeks or later in pregnancy unless a health care professional specifically advises it.

## One-page visit summary

Bring this instead of the full diary.

```text
Period pain and cramp summary

Date range:
Last period start date:
Worst pain day and score:
Main pain location(s):
What came with the pain:
Daily impact:
Medicine or comfort steps already used:
Main question:
```

Mayo Clinic appointment prep for menstrual cramps asks people to write down period dates and cramp severity. Mayo Clinic also says to see a health care professional if menstrual cramps disrupt life every month, symptoms get worse, or severe cramps start after age 25.

## Questions to bring

Pick the ones that fit.

- Could my cycle timing matter for this pain?
- What other causes should we consider?
- Does the location or pattern change what you check?
- Should I keep tracking for more than one cycle?
- What symptoms mean I should call sooner?
- Should I bring the full log or a short summary?
- What details can I leave out of an export?
