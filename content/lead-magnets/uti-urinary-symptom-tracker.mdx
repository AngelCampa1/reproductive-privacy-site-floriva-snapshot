Burning, urgency, peeing often, and bladder pressure can show up on their own. They can also show up near sex, near your period, or over and over.

This page is one tracker for all of it. It does not diagnose a UTI, cystitis, pregnancy, or any other cause. It helps you bring clear notes and good questions to a clinician.

It is not medical advice.

## Call-out symptoms

Contact a clinician promptly if any of these fit.

- [ ] Fever
- [ ] Back or side pain
- [ ] Nausea or vomiting
- [ ] Blood in urine
- [ ] Pregnancy or possible pregnancy
- [ ] Symptoms feel severe
- [ ] Symptoms are getting worse

If you feel very sick, seek urgent care.

Mayo Clinic says back or side pain, high fever, chills, nausea, and vomiting can happen when the kidneys are affected.

## What not to assume

Do not assume:

- A period means urinary symptoms are normal.
- A symptom near sex or ovulation has a clear cause.
- A past UTI means this is the same thing.
- A log can replace a urine test.
- An app can tell you what treatment you need.

The goal is clearer notes, not self-diagnosis.

## Questions to ask

Pick the questions that fit your situation.

- Do my symptoms need a urine test today?
- Do I need a urine culture?
- Do my records fit recurrent UTI?
- Could another cause fit my symptoms, such as cystitis, pregnancy, or a pelvic floor issue?
- Should pregnancy change what we check?
- What symptoms mean I should call sooner?
- Do you need my full history, or is this summary enough?
- Should I see gynecology, primary care, or urology?

Do not use this page to choose antibiotics or change medicine. Ask your clinician what care fits you.

## Privacy note

Bladder notes can include sex timing, pregnancy worry, urine culture results, medicine, and location clues. Share the smallest useful record.

Ask:

- Do you need my full app history?
- Is this summary enough?
- Will these notes go in my chart?
- Can I leave out private notes not tied to care?

Floriva can keep dates and notes on your device. Paper works too. No app can promise full privacy. You still choose what to type, export, screenshot, print, or share.
