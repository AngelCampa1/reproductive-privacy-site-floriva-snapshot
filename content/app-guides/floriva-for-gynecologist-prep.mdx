## Why Cycle Data Helps in Gynecologist Appointments

Your clinician may ask about cycle dates, bleeding, pain, and repeat symptoms. A short summary can make that easier to discuss. The hard part is having those notes in a usable form when you arrive.

"My periods are pretty heavy" is less useful to a clinician than "my periods last six days, I go through four to five heavy pads on days two and three, and I've had this pattern for at least six months." The first is a description. The second is data.

Floriva's on-device logging helps you build the second version while core tracking stays on your device.

## What Consistency Actually Means

Consistency in cycle logging doesn't mean logging every possible field every day. It means logging the most important fields on the days they're relevant, for every cycle.

If it helps, you can note period start date, period end date, flow, pain, and spotting. Bring what you have.

Several cycles of simple notes can be easier to discuss than a long record with many gaps.

## The Privacy Advantage for Appointment Notes

When you build appointment notes in Floriva, core tracking stays on your device. You decide what to bring to the appointment. Screenshots, exports, backups, shared phones, and messages can still create copies.

[Period tracker data is not always covered by HIPAA](/resources/guides/period-tracker-hipaa/). Data you share from cloud-based apps may be governed by that app's privacy policy, not medical privacy law. If you share Floriva data, a summary you choose from on-device records can limit extra detail.
