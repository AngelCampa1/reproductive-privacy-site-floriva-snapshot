## What "anonymous tracking" actually means here

True anonymity is a spectrum, not a binary. This guide covers the practical steps to minimize the data trail from Floriva, which is already small by design, without overstating what is achievable.

What you can control with these steps:
- Whether any data leaves your device through Floriva's infrastructure (it cannot. The app has no sync and no network calls)
- Whether health platform integrations route your data through Apple or Google ecosystems
- Whether device backups include app data accessible on a shared account
- Whether someone with physical access to your phone can open the app

What you cannot control through app settings:
- Whether the App Store or Google Play logged that you downloaded a period tracking app (they do, against your account)
- Whether your device's operating system logs that the app was opened

Most users are concerned about data brokers and app companies, intimate partner surveillance, or legal requests. These steps address the first two significantly and the third as much as app settings allow.

## The default Floriva setup is already more private than most apps

Floriva keeps core data on your device and requires no account for core tracking. That gives it a smaller exposure surface than cloud-connected trackers before you change any settings. Flo, Clue, and similar apps store data on servers by default. Flo's 2021 FTC enforcement action documented data sharing with Facebook and Google. Floriva has no sync at all.

The steps below address the remaining surface area.

## Step-by-step anonymous setup

### No account, no name

Floriva does not require an account. This is the most important setting: there is no account to require, and no sync or cloud backup prompt to skip.

If the app asks for a name during onboarding, enter whatever you like. The name is for app use. It does not create a user profile for core tracking.

### Health platform connections

Apple Health and Google Fit are platform-level health data aggregators. Connecting Floriva to either of them routes your cycle data through Apple's or Google's data infrastructure, which has its own data handling terms and can be accessed by apps with health platform permissions.

For anonymous tracking, keep Floriva isolated. Do not accept the prompt to connect to a health platform. If you have already connected it, revoke the permission: on iOS in Settings, Health, Apps, Floriva; on Android in Settings, Google Fit or Health Connect.

### Cloud backup exclusion

Device backups are a frequently overlooked exposure point, and the two platforms behave differently here.

On Android, Floriva excludes its own data from Google's device backup, so this is handled for you automatically.

On iOS, that protection does not currently exist inside the app: if you have iCloud Backup turned on for your device, Floriva's local data is included in that backup like most other apps' data. There is no per-app toggle that removes just Floriva from it. If your iCloud backup account is shared with or accessible to someone else, the reliable options are to turn off iCloud Backup for the device entirely, or to use a separate Apple ID that only you control.

### Lock screen and notification hygiene

Your device's lock screen is the primary barrier between your Floriva data and anyone with your device. Use a passcode or biometric authentication. Do not use a pattern unlock, which can be observed.

Floriva's push notifications may display cycle reminders or tracking prompts on your lock screen. If you share a home with others or use your device in shared spaces, review your notification privacy settings. On iOS: Settings, Notifications, Floriva, Show Previews, When Unlocked. This prevents notification content from appearing on the lock screen.

## What this setup protects against

After completing these steps, your Floriva setup:

- Stores no readable cycle records on Floriva servers
- Creates no account linkable to your identity
- Routes no data through Apple Health or Google Fit ecosystems
- Excludes app data from device cloud backup
- Requires device authentication to view

The remaining exposure is physical: someone with access to your unlocked device can open the app. An additional app lock (step 5 above) addresses this without requiring a Floriva-specific feature.
