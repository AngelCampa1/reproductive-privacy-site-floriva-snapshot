## Abortion Law Status in North Dakota

Abortion is banned in North Dakota under SB 2150, enacted in 2023. That law replaced the state's earlier trigger law after legal challenges. The ban allows abortion only in cases of rape, incest, or to save the life of the mother. North Dakota borders Minnesota, which protects abortion access. Minnesota is a common destination for North Dakota residents seeking care.

## Period Tracker Data Risk in North Dakota

North Dakota has no consumer data privacy law. Abortion is banned. App companies that store your cycle data, pregnancy logs, or location history have no state-level legal obligation to protect that information from law enforcement requests. Location data is especially sensitive here. Travel to Minnesota is a common pattern, and that travel history could become relevant to a prosecution.

## Risk Classification: Danger Zone

North Dakota combines a criminal abortion ban, no comprehensive data privacy law, and no shield law. The Center for American Progress documented 210 pregnancy-related prosecutions in the year following Dobbs (June 2022 to June 2023), the highest single-year total ever recorded. Prosecutors in states like North Dakota face minimal legal barriers to obtaining reproductive health data through subpoenas, court orders, or data broker purchases.

## Federal Protection Status

The HIPAA Reproductive Privacy Rule would have blocked disclosure of protected health information in abortion investigations. A federal court in Texas vacated it nationwide on June 18, 2025. HHS did not appeal. Biden-era executive orders directing FTC and HHS action on reproductive data privacy were revoked on January 24, 2025. A ClearDATA/Harris Poll survey (May 2023, n=2,053) found 81% of Americans wrongly believe HIPAA covers health apps. It does not. Period tracker apps are not healthcare providers. They fall entirely outside HIPAA regardless of state. There is no federal floor for reproductive health data protection.
