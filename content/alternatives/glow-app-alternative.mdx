## What Glow Doesn't Document

Glow is a period and fertility tracker that has been available since 2013. It offers cycle tracking, fertility prediction, pregnancy tracking, and community features. The app has a substantial user base and broad feature coverage.

What Glow does not offer is a documented privacy-first architecture. The app is cloud-based. Its privacy policy does not clearly commit to avoiding data sales to third parties. There is no on-device storage model and no published commitment to zero-knowledge design.

## Documented Privacy Failures

Glow has a specific enforcement and breach history. In September 2020, Glow settled with the California Attorney General for $250,000. Investigators found the "Partner Connect" feature automatically granted data access to anyone who sent a linking request, without the recipient's confirmation. This exposed intimate health data including miscarriage and abortion history to unauthorized parties. Consumer Reports originally discovered the vulnerability in July 2016. The gap between discovery and settlement was four years.

In October 2024, security researcher Ovi Liber discovered an API bug exposing personal data of approximately 25 million Glow users, including names, age groups, location data, and user-uploaded images.

Mozilla's Privacy Not Included review found Glow's privacy claims were "easily shown to be false" regarding data sharing practices. The pattern is consistent: privacy claims that do not hold up under independent review.

## The Architecture Gap

Period tracker data is sensitive. It includes cycle dates, fertility status, pregnancy information, and symptom logs that can reveal health conditions. When this data is stored on a company's servers, three risks apply regardless of the company's intentions: the company can change its privacy policy, a legal process can compel data disclosure, and a security breach can expose the data.

Glow's cloud architecture means all three risks apply. The company has not published technical documentation that would indicate mitigation of any of them.

## How Floriva Compares

Floriva keeps core records on your device. There is no central cycle database to sell, breach, or produce from our systems. The app has no sync, so we receive nothing. For users who want Glow's fertility and cycle tracking features without accepting readable cloud storage, Floriva is the architectural alternative.
