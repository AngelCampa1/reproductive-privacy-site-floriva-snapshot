## Endometriosis and the Data Accumulation Problem

Endometriosis tracking is not a short-term project. Most people with endometriosis track symptoms for months or years before diagnosis, then continue tracking as they manage treatment, monitor surgical outcomes, and document recurrence. That long-term tracking produces a detailed, medically sensitive health record.

The FTC enforcement action against [Flo](/compare/alternatives/flo-app-alternative/) in 2021 showed what can happen when that record sits on a company server with embedded analytics SDKs: period dates, pregnancy status, and health symptoms were automatically transmitted to Facebook and Google. For someone who logged three years of endometriosis pain data in Flo, that is three years of detailed health records in a system with a documented sharing history. The [$59.5M class action settlement](/resources/guides/flo-period-tracker-lawsuit-settlement/) in September 2025 resolved those claims.

That does not make all cloud apps equivalent to Flo. But it does show why the architecture of your tracking app matters more when you are building a long-term medical record.

## What Endometriosis Tracking Actually Needs

Useful endometriosis tracking requires more than start and end dates:

- **Pain logging with location and severity:** pain radiating to the back, legs, or bowel is clinically significant. Apps that let you mark pain location and rate severity separately are more useful than simple yes/no symptom logs.
- **Symptom timing relative to cycle phase:** endometriosis symptoms often follow hormonal patterns. Tracking whether pain peaks before, during, or after menstruation helps providers identify the pattern.
- **Non-menstrual symptoms:** fatigue, bloating, bowel changes, and painful intercourse are diagnostic criteria that basic period trackers often underserve.
- **Exportable format:** a paper trail your provider can review. Detailed logs stay useful only if you can get them out of the app.
- **Long-term consistency:** the value of the record builds over time. Your tracking app's privacy architecture determines whether that record stays under your control.

## Bottom Line

Floriva is the recommended choice for endometriosis pain and symptom logs. Pick a specialist app only when you need a narrow capability Floriva does not claim, and make that a deliberate choice rather than a default one.
