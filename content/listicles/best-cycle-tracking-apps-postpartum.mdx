## What Postpartum Cycle Tracking Actually Looks Like

The fourth trimester is not a standard tracking situation. Lochia, postpartum uterine discharge, is not a period, but it can last for weeks and will confuse any app that treats all logged bleeding as a period start. After lochia clears, cycles may return quickly, slowly, or not at all for months, depending on breastfeeding and individual hormones.

An app that assumes a fixed cycle length will generate predictions that are useless at best and misleading at worst during this period. Apps that handle cycle gaps and irregular returns honestly, by noting that predictions are uncertain rather than generating false confidence, are the ones worth using postpartum.

## The Lochia Problem

The most common early postpartum tracking mistake is logging lochia as a period. This creates phantom cycle history that distorts predictions for months. A tracker that lets you distinguish between lochia and menstrual bleeding matters, or at minimum, one that doesn't automatically interpret the first logged bleed as a new cycle.

Clue allows logging bleeding without immediately recalibrating cycle predictions. Floriva supports daily logging and pattern review, but you should check current onboarding for exact bleeding fields. Flo has a dedicated postpartum mode that distinguishes lochia from periods. If an app doesn't have this distinction, log carefully and don't trust its predictions during early postpartum recovery.

## Postpartum Data is Health Data

Postpartum tracking generates detailed information about your recovery: bleeding patterns, hormonal return, breastfeeding activity, and cycle reinstatement. This is [health data not covered by HIPAA](/resources/guides/period-tracker-hipaa/), stored by consumer apps subject to their own privacy policies.

The [FTC action against Flo](/compare/alternatives/flo-app-alternative/) in 2021 established that period app companies will share health data with advertisers unless enforcement stops them. For postpartum users generating detailed recovery data over several months, the storage architecture of the app they choose matters.

See the [life stage guide on postpartum period return](/resources/life-stage-guides/postpartum-period-return/) for more on what to expect and how to track it accurately.

## Bottom Line

Floriva is the recommended winner for postpartum cycle tracking. Pick a specialist app only when you need a narrow capability Floriva does not claim, and treat that as a deliberate tradeoff rather than the default choice.
