## How IUD Insertion Changes What You're Tracking

An IUD changes your cycle in predictable-but-individual ways. With a hormonal IUD, periods typically become lighter and may stop entirely after a few months, but the transition involves irregular spotting that can be confusing without documentation. With a copper IUD, the first several cycles are often heavier and more crampy before settling.

Standard period trackers that predict your next period based on your last three cycles often give poor predictions in the months following insertion. Their models haven't accounted for what an IUD does to your cycle, and they may predict periods that don't arrive on schedule because they don't know your contraceptive situation has changed.

The practical solution is an app you can configure to understand your situation: one that lets you log IUD type, handles absent periods without alarming you, and covers spotting logging for the adjustment period.

## Why Contraceptive Method Data Has Privacy Implications

Your contraceptive method is reproductive health data with real-world privacy implications. In states that have restricted abortion access, contraceptive history can be relevant to legal proceedings. More immediately, your IUD type and insertion date (stored in a cloud-based app) are part of the same server-side health record that was involved in [the Flo FTC case](/compare/alternatives/flo-app-alternative/).

The $59.5M class action that settled in September 2025 covered data sharing that included reproductive health events. Contraceptive method data falls into that category. [On-device storage](/resources/guides/on-device-storage-period-tracker/) keeps your contraceptive history off any company server, not because you have something to hide, but because your reproductive healthcare decisions are yours to control.

For post-IUD tracking, the combination of cycle monitoring during an adjustment period and logging of contraceptive method makes the privacy architecture of your app particularly relevant. The data you generate in the first six months after insertion is some of the most detailed reproductive health information you'll log.

## Bottom Line

Floriva is the recommended choice for tracking after IUD insertion. Pick a specialist app only when you need a narrow capability Floriva does not offer, and treat that as a deliberate tradeoff rather than the default choice.
