## Spot On Is Free and Mission-Aligned

Planned Parenthood built Spot On to offer free cycle and contraception tracking alongside reproductive health education. The app covers cycle prediction, contraception method tracking, and sexual health information grounded in Planned Parenthood's clinical programs.

For users who want Planned Parenthood's health content built into their period tracker, Spot On is the one free app that delivers it.

## The Account and Server Problem

Spot On requires an account. That account links your email address, and your identity, to a Planned Parenthood digital service and to your cycle data on their servers.

This creates two categories of data exposure.

**Standard subpoena risk**: Like any server-based period tracker, Spot On's user data is accessible via court order. Prosecutors in states with abortion restrictions have sought period tracking data in criminal proceedings. Server-based storage is the exposure pathway.

**Organizational risk**: Planned Parenthood is not a neutral tech company. It is a healthcare provider that faces state legislative action, funding restrictions, and civil legal pressure in multiple US states. This adds a risk layer that is different from a typical tech company facing the same subpoena. An organization under sustained legal pressure in some jurisdictions has a different risk profile for user data.

## What Floriva Offers Instead

Floriva is a paid app with no free tier that stores data on your device with no account required. No Planned Parenthood association, no account email, no readable central record.

If the Planned Parenthood health content is what draws you to Spot On, that content is also available directly on plannedparenthood.org without creating an app account.
