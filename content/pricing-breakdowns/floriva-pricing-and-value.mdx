## Floriva's Pricing Model

Floriva has a single paid tier, offered as monthly, annual, or lifetime (one-time) billing. There is no free tier. Current pricing for each option is shown in the app before you subscribe.

All plans include local-first cycle tracking, period logging, daily health notes, pattern review on the device, and privacy-focused export or backup options where supported. Check current Floriva onboarding for the exact fields and export options available in your app version. Floriva does not monetize your data for advertising or data broker sales.

## The Hidden Costs of "Free"

The FTC charged Flo Health in 2021 because its free tier was paid for with user data. Health data was transmitted to Facebook Analytics and AppsFlyer despite Flo stating it would not sell personal data.

The actual cost of a "free" period tracker with these practices:

**Advertising profile building:** Health conditions, reproductive status, and cycle timing become advertising signals. If you use Flo's free tier and have disclosed that you have PCOS, are trying to conceive, or track pregnancy, this information may shape the advertising you see across the web.

**Data broker sales and sharing:** Health data collected by non-HIPAA apps can legally be sold to data brokers in most US states. Data broker data is used for insurance risk assessment, employer screening, credit decisions, and targeted advertising.

**Legal exposure:** Data held on US cloud servers is accessible to law enforcement via subpoena in many cases without a warrant. The "free" app creates a trail of your reproductive health and fertility data that sits on a company's server indefinitely.

## What You Are Not Paying With Floriva

**No advertising profile:** No advertiser learns about your cycle timing, fertility status, or reproductive health conditions from Floriva.

**No data broker data:** Floriva cannot sell what it does not have.

**Less company-side subpoena exposure:** Floriva has no readable central cycle database to produce in response to a company records request. Device access is a separate legal path.

**Less company-side breach exposure:** A Floriva server incident should not expose your readable core cycle history because those records are not stored in a readable central database.

## Pricing in Context

Floriva is a paid subscription with no free tier. What differs from Clue Plus and Natural Cycles:

**vs. Clue Plus:** Clue offers automatic cloud backup and multi-device sync. Floriva centers local-first storage with no sync, plus an encrypted backup file you export and move yourself. Clue has GDPR protections as a German company. Floriva's protection is architectural: no readable central cycle database. For many users, Clue's GDPR protections may be enough. For users worried about company-held records, GDPR does not change that data exists on Clue's servers.

**vs. Natural Cycles:** Natural Cycles is FDA-cleared for contraceptive use, a different product category with clinical backing for contraception. Floriva does not offer contraceptive algorithm analysis. These serve different purposes.

**vs. Flo Premium:** Both offer advanced period tracking features. The difference is architecture: Flo's data lives on US servers; Floriva's lives on your device.

## Who Gets the Most Value From Floriva Premium

**People for whom reproductive health data privacy is a practical concern:** Those in US states with restrictive reproductive health laws, people in immigration-sensitive situations, people who have experienced stalking or domestic surveillance, people who want to minimize digital health footprints.

**Cycle tracking enthusiasts who want longitudinal pattern analysis:** People who track BBT, cervical mucus, and LH strips across multiple cycles and want to analyze phase patterns over time.

**Privacy-conscious users who do not want to be the product:** Anyone who has read the Flo FTC complaint and decided that "free" apps have costs they do not want to pay.

## What This Means for Floriva Users

The pricing comparison is only part of the value equation. The other part is the cost you are not paying: no data used against you in advertising, no trail for legal compulsion, no breach exposure from server records. For some users, this makes the subscription a clear choice. For users without these concerns, the feature comparison with Clue or Flo determines the choice.
