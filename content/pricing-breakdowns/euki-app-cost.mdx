## Euki Is Genuinely Free and Genuinely Private

Euki charges nothing and does not monetize user data. No ad tier, no data licensing, no paid upgrade. Reproductive health nonprofits fund it. It uses on-device storage and requires no account.

For users who need a free period tracker with real privacy, Euki is the strongest option available. It is a good app.

## The Funding Model Question

Euki's strength is also its limitation. Nonprofit funding means development is driven by grant cycles, not user demand. When a grant ends, development can slow or stop. The app can fall behind on iOS and Android updates. Features that paying users would fund, like an encrypted backup file for switching phones, are not priorities in a grant model.

This is not a criticism of Euki's mission. It is how nonprofit software works under budget constraints.

Several reproductive health apps have gone stale or stopped working when their funding ended. Users who built their tracking history in those apps lost it.

## What Floriva's Subscription Adds

Floriva uses the same approach as Euki: data stays on your device, no account required, nothing on a server, no sync. The subscription funds:

- An encrypted backup file you can export and move to a new phone yourself, protected by a passphrase Floriva never sees
- Active iOS and Android development
- Long-term product continuity

If $0 is the requirement, Euki is a solid choice. If a paid subscription works for you, Floriva adds that backup file and a sustainable roadmap.
