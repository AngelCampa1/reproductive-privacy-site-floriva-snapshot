## The Market: $0 to $12.99/mo

The period tracker market splits into three pricing categories.

**Free, data-monetized**: Flo's free tier, historically. The app is free because user health data funds advertising revenue. The FTC documented this with Flo in 2021. It is the oldest business model in consumer tech applied to reproductive health data.

**Free, mission-funded**: Euki, Drip, Spot On. These apps charge nothing because a nonprofit or open-source community funds them. No data monetization, but sustainability depends on grants and community maintenance.

**Paid subscription**: Flo Premium ($4.99/mo), Clue Plus ($9.99/mo), Stardust ($7.99/mo), Glow ($9.99/mo), Natural Cycles ($12.99/mo), Floriva (paid, no free tier). The subscription covers server infrastructure, app development, and in some cases FDA clearance and clinical research.

Note: Natural Cycles raised prices in June 2025. The $12.99/mo figure listed here was current at original publication. Check current rates directly.

## The Two Verified Data-Sharing Incidents

Two apps in this comparison have documented FTC enforcement actions.

**Flo Health (2021)**: The FTC found Flo shared health data, including period dates, pregnancy status, and symptoms, with Facebook and Google advertising SDKs without user consent. The FTC issued a consent order. In September 2025, a combined $59.5M class action resolved claims by affected users.

**Premom / Easy Healthcare (2023)**: The FTC found Premom shared sensitive health and location data with Chinese analytics firms Umeng and Jiguang without adequate disclosure.

These are the only two period tracker companies with FTC enforcement on record for data sharing as of early 2026.

## What the Comparison Table Reveals

The table above shows that "free" and "server-based" go together in almost every case. The exceptions are Euki and Drip, which are free and on-device, but both have structural limitations: no sync, platform restrictions, or grant-dependent continuity.

Floriva is one of the few trackers that combines on-device storage, iOS and Android support, and an encrypted backup file for moving to a new phone, as a paid subscription with no free tier.
