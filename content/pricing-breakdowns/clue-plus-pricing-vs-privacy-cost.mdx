## Understanding Clue's Pricing Structure

[Clue](/compare/alternatives/clue-alternative-no-cloud/) has two tiers: free and Plus at $9.99/mo or $59.99/yr. The free tier works for core period tracking. No ads, solid symptom logging, cycle predictions. Plus adds health analysis: birth control tracking, detailed cycle reports, symptom correlation, and partner sharing.

Compared to [Flo](/compare/alternatives/flo-app-alternative/), Clue wins at the free tier. Flo's free tier is ad-supported. Flo shared reproductive health data with Facebook and Google before the FTC intervened. Clue's free tier has no ads. You are not paying for the app with your data.

## What You Are Paying For With Plus

Plus features are health monitoring tools, not privacy upgrades. Cycle health reports match your logged symptoms to your cycle phase. Birth control tracking adds reminders and logs your method. Symptom correlation shows patterns in your data. Partner sharing lets someone see your cycle predictions.

These features help people managing a health condition, tracking fertility, or coordinating with a partner. If you mainly want to know when your period is coming and log basic symptoms, the free tier is enough.

## The Privacy Picture at Both Tiers

Clue's privacy setup is the same at both tiers. You create an account. Your cycle data, symptom logs, and health info are stored on Clue's servers in Berlin. GDPR protects your data from commercial misuse. A valid court order can still compel Clue to produce it.

Paying for Plus does not change any of this. The $9.99/mo buys features, not a different data structure. If you care about structural data privacy, the upgrade decision and the privacy decision are separate.

## What GDPR Does Not Protect Against

GDPR is a strong commercial privacy law. It stops Clue from selling your data, sharing it with advertisers, or using it in ways not in its privacy policy. It does not protect against law enforcement subpoenas or data breaches.

US authorities can request data from European companies through international legal agreements. GDPR does not block this. For most users, GDPR protection is enough. For users in US states with criminal abortion restrictions, where prosecutors have sought period tracking data, the [subpoena pathway](/resources/guides/can-police-access-period-tracker-data/) is a real concern, especially in states like [Texas](/period-tracker-privacy/reproductive-data-privacy-laws-texas/).

## Architectural Privacy vs Policy Privacy

Clue's privacy is policy-based. The company commits to not misusing your data, and German law backs that. Floriva's privacy is architectural. Your data lives on your device and never reaches a server. No policy change, court order, or breach can expose data that does not exist on a server.

## Comparing to On-Device Alternatives

Floriva is a paid app with no free tier, using on-device storage and no account required. Floriva has fewer features than Clue Plus, especially on health reporting. But for users not using health analysis and focused on where their data lives, the privacy architecture is stronger regardless of which plan you compare.
