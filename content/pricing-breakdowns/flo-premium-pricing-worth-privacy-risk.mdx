## What Flo Premium Actually Includes

Flo has three tiers: a free ad-supported tier, Premium at $4.99/mo, and Premium Plus at $9.99/mo. The free tier covers basic cycle tracking and predictions but shows ads. Premium removes ads, adds health reports, AI chat, and Anonymous Mode.

Anonymous Mode is the privacy-relevant addition. When you turn it on, Flo replaces your name and email with a random identifier in its systems. This makes your data harder to link back to you within Flo's infrastructure.

## What Anonymous Mode Does Not Do

Anonymous Mode does not change where your data lives. Your cycle history, symptom logs, and health data stay on Flo's servers. It makes your data harder to identify. It does not remove your data from Flo's servers.

A court order for your data can still apply to Anonymous Mode accounts. The data is there. Anonymous Mode is an obfuscation layer, not an absence of data.

This matters most in US states with abortion restrictions. Even pseudonymous data on a server can be legally compelled.

## The Enforcement History Problem

In 2021, the FTC found that Flo shared user health data with advertising partners. Flo's privacy policy had said it would not do this. Flo responded by creating Anonymous Mode and changing its data practices.

The question is: if the previous privacy promises were not kept, why would the new ones hold? The business model has not fundamentally changed. The incentive to monetize user data is still there.

In September 2025, a combined $59.5M class action settlement resolved claims by affected users. On August 1, 2025, a jury separately found Meta liable for accessing user data from the Flo app.

## Comparing the Actual Cost

Flo Premium at $4.99/mo buys ad removal, health features, and a privacy control layer on top of server-based storage. Floriva is a paid app with no free tier that provides on-device storage as the default, with no ad layer to remove and no privacy mode to unlock. For users whose primary concern is data privacy rather than feature depth, the architecture is stronger regardless of price.
