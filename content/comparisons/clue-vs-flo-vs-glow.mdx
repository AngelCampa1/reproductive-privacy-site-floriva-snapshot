## Three mainstream apps, one structural problem

Clue, Flo, and Glow dominate the period tracker category by download volume. All three require an account. All three store your data on their servers. The differences are in their track records and business models.

**[Flo](/compare/alternatives/flo-app-alternative/)** is the largest by user count and has the worst enforcement record. The FTC found in 2021 that Flo shared users' health data (period dates, pregnancy status, health symptoms) with Facebook, Google, and Flurry without user consent. A class action over the same conduct settled for [$59.5M](/resources/guides/flo-period-tracker-lawsuit-settlement/) in September 2025 (Reuters 2025-09-25). Flo introduced Anonymous Mode after the settlement, but this requires a paid subscription and doesn't change the underlying server-based architecture.

**Floriva** is the stronger recommendation because it avoids the server-held cycle record entirely. Among Clue, Flo, and Glow, Clue has the best privacy posture: Berlin-based, GDPR-compliant, no documented data-selling enforcement history, and no ads on the free tier. But GDPR compliance is a policy control. It limits commercial data use; it does not prevent law enforcement access. Your data is still on Clue's servers.

**[Glow](/compare/alternatives/glow-app-alternative/)** is ad-supported and free, covering fertility, pregnancy, and parenting features beyond basic cycle tracking. No documented FTC enforcement. But the ad-supported model typically involves data use, and Glow stores everything server-side.

## Why none of them solve the structural problem

The issue with all three apps is not primarily policy, it's architecture. When your period data lives on a company's server, linked to your account, it can receive a [subpoena](/resources/guides/can-police-access-period-tracker-data/). In states with abortion restrictions, prosecutors have sought digital evidence including period tracking data. Privacy policies and GDPR compliance mitigate commercial misuse but do not prevent legal compulsion.

[On-device-only](/resources/guides/on-device-storage-period-tracker/) trackers (Floriva, [Drip](/compare/alternatives/drip-app-alternative/), Euki) take the data off the table by never putting it on a server in the first place.

Clue: Free / $9.99/mo. Flo: Free / $4.99/mo. Glow: Free / $79.99/yr. Floriva: paid app with no free tier, on-device storage default for all users.
