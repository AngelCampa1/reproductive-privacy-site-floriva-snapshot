FAM is not a general-purpose period tracking use case. It is a specific practice that requires specific features. An app that is excellent for period prediction may be useless for FAM, and vice versa.

The features that matter for FAM:

1. **BBT logging with two decimal place support**, visual display of the biphasic pattern across the cycle
2. **Cervical mucus notation**, at minimum D/S/C/EW categories; ideally with sensation notation
3. **LH strip logging**, to complement BBT and mucus observation
4. **Chart visualization**, the temperature graph needs to be readable for cover line identification
5. **Cycle continuity**, the chart should persist across cycle transitions without losing context
6. **Rules guidance (optional)**, either applying STM rules or educating users to apply them

## The Apps

### Natural Cycles (FDA-Cleared Contraceptive)

**What it does for FAM:** Processes daily BBT input through a proprietary algorithm. Designates each day as green (not fertile, unprotected sex OK) or red (fertile, protection needed). Adjusts predictions as more data is accumulated.

**What it doesn't do:** It doesn't teach you to read your own chart. It's a medical device, not a learning tool. You're trusting the algorithm's determination rather than developing your own ability to interpret fertility signs.

**Best for:** People who want algorithmic contraception with FDA backing and don't need to develop independent FAM skills. GDPR-protected as a Swedish company.

**Privacy:** Cloud-based, account required. EU jurisdiction.

**Effectiveness:** 98% perfect use, 93% typical use. Clinical data supports contraceptive use.

### Kindara

**What it does for FAM:** Full BBT charting with visual biphasic pattern display. Cervical mucus notation. LH strip logging. Cover line visual support. Notes per cycle day. Data export. Was the dominant self-learner FAM app for years.

**What it doesn't do:** Apply STM rules automatically (which is actually correct for FAM learning, you should apply rules yourself). Verify current development status before relying on it; Kindara's development activity has been inconsistent.

**Best for:** Self-taught FAM learners who want full charting features without algorithmic interference.

**Privacy:** US cloud, account required. Standard US risk profile.

### Read Your Body

**What it does for FAM:** Designed specifically for evidence-based FAM practice. Full BBT charting, cervical mucus, LH strips. Built-in education about fertility sign interpretation. Strong for users learning FAM for the first time alongside a course or book.

**What it doesn't do:** Make fertility determinations for you, you're learning to read your own data. This is by design.

**Best for:** People learning FAM with serious intent who want the app to support their learning rather than replace their judgment.

**Privacy:** Offers local storage option with account optional for sync. Better than most cloud apps on this dimension.

### Clue

**What it does for FAM:** Basic fertility tracking with period prediction, some symptom logging, and LH strip support. Handles irregular cycles better than Flo. Good data export.

**What it doesn't do:** Support full FAM practice. BBT logging exists but the chart quality isn't designed for cover-line analysis. Not a FAM tool; it's a general period tracking app that can accommodate some fertility awareness use.

**Best for:** FAM beginners who want to start logging before investing in a dedicated FAM app.

**Privacy:** German company, GDPR. Better than US apps.

### Floriva

**What it does for FAM:** Full BBT logging and visualization. Cervical mucus notation (D/S/C/EW). LH strip result logging. Cycle phase display. Offline-first with no account requirement. You apply FAM rules to your own data.

**What it doesn't do:** Apply STM rules algorithmically. No certified instructor integration (though you can work with any instructor using your exported chart data).

**Best for:** FAM practitioners who prioritize privacy above all else and are comfortable interpreting their own charts. Works for both avoiding and achieving pregnancy with correct rules application.

**Privacy:** Local-first, no account. Highest privacy architecture available for FAM.

## FAM + Privacy Combined

If both FAM feature depth and privacy are priorities:
1. **Floriva**, highest privacy with full FAM features; requires learning to interpret your own chart
2. **Read Your Body**, strongest FAM features with better-than-average privacy; requires cloud account for sync
3. **Kindara**, strong FAM features, US cloud, verify current development status

If FDA clearance for contraceptive use is your priority: Natural Cycles is in a separate category from all of the above.

## What This Means for Floriva Users

FAM tracking in Floriva is the practice of recording your observations. BBT, cervical mucus, LH strips, and reading the pattern they produce. Floriva doesn't tell you when you're fertile; it shows you the data that tells you when you're fertile, when you learn how to read it. That's not a limitation, that's what FAM is.
