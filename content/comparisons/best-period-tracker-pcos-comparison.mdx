PCOS is the most common cause of irregular periods, affecting an estimated 8-13% of people with ovaries. It's also the use case where standard period tracking apps fail most consistently, because most period apps are built around a regular 28-day model that doesn't fit PCOS cycle patterns.

## What PCOS Tracking Actually Needs

A useful PCOS period tracker must handle:

**Irregular cycle lengths:** PCOS cycles can range from under 21 days to over 90 days in the same person, in the same year. An algorithm that predicts based on average cycle length will be wrong consistently.

**Ovulation confirmation, not prediction:** In PCOS, ovulation may occur but at unpredictable times, or not at all. LH strips can show multiple surges before a successful ovulation, or surges that don't result in ovulation. BBT tracking is the only way to confirm ovulation actually occurred.

**Androgen symptom correlation:** Acne, hirsutism (excess hair), hair thinning, and fatigue after meals are PCOS-relevant symptoms. Tracking these against cycle phase and LH surge timing can reveal patterns that help with management.

**Data export:** For clinical visits, 3+ months of logged data should be exportable in a format a doctor can read. This is frequently missing from consumer period apps.

## App Comparison for PCOS

### Clue

**Strengths for PCOS:** Adapts cycle predictions to your actual logged history (doesn't force a 28-day model). Handles irregular cycles better than Flo. Allows custom symptom logging. Clean export options. GDPR-protected as a German company.

**Weaknesses for PCOS:** Limited BBT integration. LH strip logging exists but is basic. No specific PCOS mode or flag for androgen symptoms.

**Privacy:** Cloud-stored, GDPR, EU jurisdiction. Better than US cloud apps.

### Flo

**Strengths for PCOS:** Irregular period mode adapts to your cycle history. Large symptom library. Doctor reports feature for clinical sharing. Now has some PCOS-specific content.

**Weaknesses for PCOS:** Algorithm still struggles with very irregular cycles (35+ day range). Prior history of SDK data sharing (addressed post-FTC but historical). US cloud storage, lower legal protection than EU apps.

**Privacy:** US cloud. Improved post-2021 but structurally the same architecture.

### Kindara

**Strengths for PCOS:** Built for fertility awareness with full BBT charting support. LH strip logging. Designed for users tracking closely. Identifies anovulatory cycle patterns well. The app most practitioners teaching FAM recommend alongside Floriva.

**Weaknesses for PCOS:** Less polished UI than Flo/Clue. Requires cloud account. Less PCOS-specific symptom tracking.

**Privacy:** US cloud, account required. Standard US app risk profile.

### Read Your Body

**Strengths for PCOS:** Designed specifically for fertility awareness method. Excellent BBT charting. LH strip logging. Teaches you to interpret your own data rather than relying on algorithm. Offline mode.

**Weaknesses for PCOS:** Steep learning curve. Not beginner-friendly for someone who hasn't studied FAM. Limited symptom tracking beyond fertility signs.

**Privacy:** Better than average; local storage option with account optional for sync.

### Floriva

**Strengths for PCOS:** Local-first storage. BBT logging with biphasic pattern visualization. LH strip result logging. Cervical mucus notation. Symptom tracking including PCOS-relevant categories. No algorithm pressure to conform to a regular cycle. Data stays on your device.

**Weaknesses for PCOS:** No multi-device sync. No automatic cloud backup (you can export an encrypted backup file yourself, but it's a manual step). Requires manual interpretation of fertility signs (not algorithmic).

**Privacy:** Highest, no readable central records, no account required.

## What Actually Matters for PCOS

For a person with PCOS who primarily wants to understand their cycle and support clinical management:
- LH strips + BBT together are more informative than any algorithm
- Any app that supports both and handles irregular cycles is workable
- Privacy considerations should factor in if reproductive health legal risk is relevant

For a person with PCOS who is trying to conceive:
- BBT confirmation of ovulation is essential
- Multiple-day LH monitoring is important (PCOS can produce multiple surges)
- The data needs to be exportable and readable by a fertility specialist

For a person with PCOS who prioritizes privacy:
- Floriva's local-first architecture eliminates server risk entirely
