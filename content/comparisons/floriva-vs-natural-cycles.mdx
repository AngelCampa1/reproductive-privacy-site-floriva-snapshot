This comparison requires a critical up-front clarification: Natural Cycles and Floriva are not competing for the same use case.

**If you want a clinically validated, FDA-cleared contraceptive method based on temperature tracking:** Natural Cycles is the appropriate product. Nothing in this comparison should be read as a reason to use an uncleared tracking tool for contraception.

**If you want the most privacy-protective cycle tracking available, for health awareness, cycle syncing, fertility monitoring, or any purpose other than FDA-cleared contraception:** Floriva's architecture is the relevant comparison.

## What Natural Cycles Actually Is

Natural Cycles is a medical device. It received FDA De Novo clearance in 2018 as the first app cleared specifically for contraceptive use. The clearance means FDA reviewed clinical data on the algorithm's accuracy and safety as a birth control method.

Key facts:
- **Typical use failure rate:** 7% per year (1 in 14 users will become pregnant per year with typical use)
- **Perfect use failure rate:** 2% per year (1 in 50 with perfect use)
- **Method:** Daily BBT measurement plus optional LH test strips, processed by a proprietary algorithm that designates days "green" (not fertile) or "red" (fertile/unprotected sex not recommended)
- **Cleared for:** Women 18+ who are not in menopause; not cleared for those who recently had a pregnancy, childbirth, or discontinuation of hormonal contraception until cycles have resumed

For comparison: the copper IUD has about 0.8% typical use failure rate; the hormonal IUD is below 0.1%. Natural Cycles is roughly comparable to other FAM methods but has the distinction of FDA clearance for contraceptive use.

## Natural Cycles' Data Privacy

Natural Cycles stores data under GDPR, which provides more specific protections than US federal law:
- Health data is a "special category" requiring explicit consent
- Users have access, correction, and deletion rights
- Cross-border data transfers to non-EU countries require adequate protections
- Violations carry potential fines of up to 4% of global revenue

Natural Cycles faced scrutiny from the Swedish Data Protection Authority (IMY) in 2019 for sharing de-identified user data with Facebook for advertising. The company was not fined but was required to update its data practices. The incident shows that GDPR compliance does not mean perfect privacy practices.

A US law enforcement subpoena to Natural Cycles would need to work through EU/US data transfer legal mechanisms. In practice, this is more complex than subpoenaing a US company, but not impossible.

## Who Each Is Right For

**Natural Cycles is right for you if:**
- You want a clinically studied natural birth control method
- You are comfortable with cloud storage under GDPR
- You prefer algorithmic guidance (green/red day system) over self-applied FAM rules
- FDA clearance matters to you as a signal of clinical validity
- You are willing to pay for contraceptive-grade analysis

**Floriva is right for you if:**
- Privacy is the top priority and you do not want cycle data on any server
- You want cycle visibility for health purposes other than contraception
- You do not want account-linked health records
- You are practicing (or want to learn) FAM with your own rule application
- You are in a legal jurisdiction where reproductive health data could be a risk

## The Thing Neither Can Do

No app, including Natural Cycles, eliminates human inconsistency in FAM-based methods. Typical use failure rates are much higher than perfect use failure rates because daily temperature measurement is imperfect, people miss days, and devices malfunction. If you rely on any temperature-based method for contraception, the consistency of your practice is the largest factor in effectiveness.

## What This Means for Floriva Users

If you are considering Floriva as a contraceptive method, that is not what it is. Use Natural Cycles, a certified FAM instructor, or a more reliable contraceptive method. If you are considering Floriva for cycle tracking, health awareness, or privacy-first fertility monitoring, the comparison to Natural Cycles is about data architecture, not about contraceptive validity.
