## Two different products competing in the same category

[Flo](/compare/alternatives/flo-app-alternative/) is a general-purpose period and symptom tracker. [Natural Cycles](/compare/alternatives/natural-cycles-alternative-privacy/) is an FDA-cleared contraceptive device that happens to be an app. The comparison only makes sense once you understand that distinction.

If contraception is your primary goal, Natural Cycles is the only app in this category with FDA De Novo clearance for contraceptive use. That clearance means the FDA reviewed clinical evidence for effectiveness, something no other period tracker has done. The trade-off is cost ($12.99/mo) and daily commitment: you take your basal body temperature every morning before getting up.

If general cycle tracking and symptom logging is your goal, Flo's free tier covers the basics. But Flo's data practices are a documented problem. The FTC found in 2021 that Flo shared health data with Facebook, Google, and Flurry without user consent. A [class action over the same conduct](/resources/guides/flo-period-tracker-lawsuit-settlement/) settled for $59.5M in September 2025 (Reuters 2025-09-25).

## The privacy gap neither closes

Both Flo and Natural Cycles require an account. Both store your data on company servers. Neither offers [on-device-only](/resources/guides/on-device-storage-period-tracker/) architecture.

This matters because server-side storage means data exists somewhere that can receive a [subpoena](/resources/guides/can-police-access-period-tracker-data/). In states with abortion restrictions, period data has been sought as evidence. GDPR compliance (Natural Cycles is Sweden-based) limits commercial misuse but does not prevent law enforcement access.

Floriva takes a different position. Core records stay on your device, no account is required for core tracking, and there is no sync to transmit anything. The FDA clearance for contraception that Natural Cycles holds is genuinely valuable. Floriva does not offer that. But for cycle tracking with less company-side legal exposure, local-first architecture gives stronger structural protection.

## Cost breakdown

Natural Cycles is the most expensive option in the space at $12.99/mo or $99.99/yr (as of original publication; check current rates at naturalcycles.com). Flo Premium is $4.99/mo or $39.99/yr. Floriva is a paid app with no free tier; plans are shown in the app. If you need FDA-cleared contraception, Natural Cycles' price is justified. If you need cycle tracking and privacy, Floriva has no readable central cycle database.
